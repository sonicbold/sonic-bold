Where should a plumbing company start?

Most plumbing companies start with Google Ads and Local Services Ads, because they put your number in front of people searching for a plumber right now. Your first 30 days of Ads and LSA management are free once campaigns go live.

Do I need to run both Google Ads and Local Services Ads?

Not always, but most shops with capacity do best with both. LSA captures the pay-per-lead slot at the top of the page, while Search Ads give keyword, ad and landing-page control.

How does the AI receptionist work?

It answers your business phone 24/7, qualifies callers, captures their details, and can book appointments directly into your schedule, covering after-hours calls, weekend emergencies, and busy-day overflow.

How much should a plumbing company spend on ads?

It depends on your service area, van count, competition, and growth goals. We recommend a sensible starting budget for your market based on real search volume in your ZIP codes, not a one-size-fits-all number.

Are the AI receptionist and call tracking included in the free 30 days?

No. The free 30 days covers Google Ads and Local Services Ads management only. Call tracking, the AI receptionist, landing pages and Google Business Profile work are separate.

Who owns the ad accounts and tracking?

You do. Google Ads, LSA, Google Business Profile, your domain and tracking stay in your name. If you leave, everything goes with you.
