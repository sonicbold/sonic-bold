Last updated: October 10, 2026 · Sonic Bold LLC

1. SMS Program Notice

This notice applies to the SMS program operated by Sonic Bold LLC across all of our phone numbers, including our toll-free and local numbers. SMS consent on our forms is optional and is not a condition of purchase or required to submit a form. If you opt in, Sonic Bold LLC may text you about offers, consultation/appointment scheduling and reminders, inquiry and missed-call replies, and account/service notifications. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. We only text people who opt in; we do not use purchased lists.

2. Data Privacy & Non-Sharing Guarantee

Mobile information, SMS opt-in data, and consent are not shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All information collected through our SMS programs is used only to deliver the messages you opted into and to operate that program.

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP. After texting STOP, you will receive one confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent. If you wish to rejoin, you may sign up again through our website or send START.

4. Customer Support & Assistance

For help or questions regarding messaging, reply HELP or contact our direct support team at marketing@sonicbold.com.

5. Rates, Fees & Message Frequency

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

6. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when filling out strategy session forms, requesting software demonstrations, or communicating with our AI receptionist and customer support agents. This may include your name, business name, phone number, email address, service area, and emergency plumbing contractor business requirements.
