Last updated: September 2026 · Sonic Bold Technologies Inc.

1. SMS Communication & A2P 10DLC Compliance Notice

We value your privacy and comply strictly with carrier standards (A2P 10DLC). When you opt-in via our contact forms, you agree to receive conversational and service-related SMS from Sonic Bold regarding your inquiry, appointment reminders, and service-related updates. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

2. Data Privacy & Non-Sharing Guarantee

Mobile information, phone numbers, and SMS consent will never be shared, rented, or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All information collected through our SMS programs is strictly used to deliver requested services, support, and platform operations.

3. Opt-Out & Unsubscribe Instructions

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP. After texting STOP, you will receive one confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent. If you wish to rejoin, you may sign up again through our website or send START.

4. Customer Support & Assistance

For help or questions regarding messaging, reply HELP or contact our direct support team at contact@sonicbold.com or through our contact hotline.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

6. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when filling out strategy session forms, requesting software demonstrations, or communicating with our AI receptionist and customer support agents. This may include your name, business name, phone number, email address, service area, and emergency plumbing contractor business requirements.
