Google Local Services Ads vs. Google Search Ads for Plumbers  Which Should You Run First?

The short answer: If you have a limited budget and want immediate phone calls, run Google Local Services Ads (LSA) first, because you only pay per valid lead. Once your LSA budget is maxed out, or if you want to target specific high-ticket jobs (like trenchless sewer repair), layer in Google Search Ads, where you pay per click but have total control over the keywords.

If you've ever searched for an "emergency plumber near me," you've probably noticed two completely different kinds of ads at the very top of Google. First, there's a "Google Guaranteed" badge with a phone number (that's LSA). Right below that, there's a standard text ad (that's Search Ads).

These are two separate products, and plumbing companies often burn money by running the wrong one first. Here is how they actually differ and which one will get you booked jobs faster.

How Local Services Ads (LSA) Work

  • Pay per lead: You are charged when a customer actually calls you, not just when they click.
  • Premium placement: They show up at the absolute top of the page, above regular search ads.
  • Built-in trust: They feature a green "Google Guaranteed" badge, which requires background checks and licensing verification.
  • The Downside: You have very little control over exactly which keywords trigger your ad, and lead quality can sometimes be hit-or-miss.

Best for: Capturing broad, high-urgency searches like "emergency plumber."

How Google Search Ads Work

  • Pay per click (PPC): You pay every time someone clicks your ad, regardless of whether they call.
  • Total control: You control the exact keywords, ad copy, and the landing page the user sees.
  • Precision targeting: You can target specific, high-ticket services like "tankless water heater installation" or "hydro jetting."
  • The Downside: They require a lot of setup. Without strict negative keywords, your budget will get wasted on DIY searchers (e.g., "how to fix a leaky faucet myself").

Which Ad Platform Should You Start With?

  • New to paid ads & want quick lead flow? Start with Local Services Ads (LSA).
  • Want to dominate a specific high-margin service? Start with Search Ads.
  • Tight monthly budget (under ,500)? Run LSA first. The pay-per-lead model limits wasted spend.
  • Have a healthy budget? Run both! LSA and Search Ads don't cannibalize each other; they dominate the top of the page together.

The Real Lever: What Happens After the Click

Both ad types will completely fail if what happens after the click is broken. Plenty of plumbing companies pour thousands of dollars into LSA and Search Ads, only to lose the jobs because their calls ring out after hours, or their landing page takes six seconds to load.

Get the ad platform right, but don't stop there. The conversion path (your website and your dispatch process) matters just as much as the click.
