FREE GOOGLE ADS AUDIT

Find out where your plumbing Google Ads are leaking booked jobs

A practical audit for plumbing companies—not a glossy report. We show where money goes, which searches waste budget, and where calls fail to become booked jobs.

Request my free Google Ads audit

If you run a plumbing company, you do not need another dashboard full of green arrows. You need to know whether your Google Ads are producing calls from people who actually need a plumber—and whether those calls turn into booked jobs.

Sonic Bold will look at your account and show you where the money is going. The audit is free. It is built for plumbing companies, not a generic local-business checklist.

What we check

Search terms. We look at the actual searches that triggered your ads. Are you paying for emergency plumbing calls, or for people looking for a course, a job, a part, a DIY answer, or a service you do not offer? Those clicks add up fast.

Negative keywords. We check whether the account is blocking the searches that waste budget. A campaign can have a good-looking cost per click and still burn money on the wrong intent if the negatives are not kept up.

Geo targeting. We check whether the ads are reaching the service area you can cover with your vans. If the targeting is loose, you may pay for calls outside your market. If it is too narrow, you may miss the neighborhoods that could fill the schedule.

Calls and booked jobs. A phone call is not the same as revenue. We look for gaps between an ad click, a call, a real conversation, and a booked job. Missed calls, slow callbacks, poor routing, and weak intake can make a decent campaign look broken—or hide a broken campaign behind a pile of calls.

Landing page and next step. We look at what a customer sees after the click. Can they tell what you do, where you work, and what to do next? Is the page helping an urgent customer call, or making them hunt for a number and a reason to trust you?

Who this is for

This is for a plumbing owner, operator, or marketing lead who is already spending on Google Ads and wants a straight answer. It is useful whether you have one van or a larger local operation, as long as the goal is more qualified calls and more booked plumbing work—not just more traffic.

You may be a fit if the account gets clicks but few calls, calls but too few booked jobs, or a monthly bill that is hard to explain. You may also be seeing duplicate leads, calls from outside the service area, or search terms that have nothing to do with your work. Those are fixable questions. They should not stay buried in a report.

What you keep

You keep the audit findings. We will show the issues we found, explain why they matter, and separate the quick fixes from the bigger decisions. You will have a short list of what to check first instead of a pile of marketing language.

We will not pretend that an account can be judged by one number. A cheap click is not a win if nobody answers the phone. A busy call log is not a win if the calls are outside your area or never become booked jobs. The point is to connect the ad, the call, and the work on the schedule.

Ask for the audit

Use the contact form to request your free Google Ads audit. Tell us a little about your plumbing company, the market you serve, and what is bothering you about the current campaigns. If you know your rough monthly ad spend or how many calls become booked jobs, include that too. If you do not know, that is fine.

We will review the details and tell you what we need to see next. No invented promises. No pressure to buy a package before anyone has looked at the account.
