Shared Leads Are Quietly Killing Your Plumbing Company's Margins

The short answer: Shared leads from platforms like Angi or HomeAdvisor kill margins because they force you into a race-to-the-bottom price war with 4 other plumbers. Instead, you should focus on exclusive leads generated from your own website and Google Ads, where you are the only company the homeowner is calling.

Here's a scenario that plays out thousands of times every single day: A frantic homeowner fills out a form on a directory site to get a plumbing quote. That single form submission gets sold—as a "lead"—to three or four different plumbing companies in the exact same area, all at the exact same time.

Suddenly, you aren't competing on the quality of your work. You are competing entirely on who can call back the fastest and who can quote the lowest price.

That is the shared lead model. It is built to maximize profit for the directory platform, not for your plumbing business.

What Shared Leads Actually Cost Your Business

  • Price races: When four contractors are quoting the exact same job, the lowest bidder usually wins—regardless of their actual skill or quality.
  • Wasted follow-up time: Your dispatch team spends hours calling back leads that were already claimed by a faster competitor five minutes ago.
  • Zero brand loyalty: The homeowner found the platform, not your brand. There is absolutely no reason for them to remember your company's name the next time they need a plumber.
  • Recurring fees for diminishing returns: You are forced to pay per lead whether or not that lead ever actually converts into a booked job.

The Power of Exclusive Leads

Exclusive leads come from marketing channels where the homeowner is reaching out to you specifically—not a directory that auctions them off to the highest bidder.

  • Google Search & Local Services Ads: Target your specific service area, ensuring the click goes straight to your phone number.
  • Dedicated Landing Pages: A site that ranks for your specific services and captures the call directly.
  • Organic SEO: Builds long-term visibility without forcing you to pay a fee for every single lead.

With an exclusive lead, the homeowner never sees a "get quotes from multiple pros" form. They see your ad, your website, your phone number—and they call you because you were the best answer to their search, not just one of five random options dumped in their inbox.

The Math That Actually Matters

A shared lead might look cheaper upfront. But if you're only closing 1 in 5 because you're stuck in a bidding war against three other contractors, your real cost-per-booked-job is often much higher than an exclusive lead. Exclusive leads close at a drastically better rate because there is no bidding war to begin with.

If you have been tracking your "cost-per-lead" instead of your "cost-per-booked-job," you have likely fallen into the shared-lead trap.

How to Make the Switch

Cutting shared leads cold-turkey can leave a scary gap in your schedule if you don't have a replacement system running yet. The companies that make this transition successfully usually run their new exclusive channels alongside their shared leads for a month or two. Once the exclusive pipeline is proven and generating calls, they phase out the shared spend entirely.
