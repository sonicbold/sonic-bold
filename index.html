WHY PLUMBING ADS FAIL

You're paying Google. But is your phone actually ringing?

Most plumbing campaigns don't fail because homeowners aren't searching. They fail because budget leaks into bad searches, the landing page doesn't convert, calls get missed, or nobody tracks which leads actually become jobs.

01 — WASTED CLICKS

Wasted Clicks

Your budget goes toward DIY searches, job seekers, and low-intent traffic instead of homeowners ready to hire an emergency plumber.

02 — WEAK CONVERSION

Weak Landing Pages

The homeowner clicks your ad, lands on a slow or confusing website without clear emergency call triggers, and hits back to call your competitor.

03 — MISSED CALLS

Missed Calls

A qualified lead calls while your techs are under a sink or after 5 PM. Nobody answers, so they call the next plumber on Google within 30 seconds.

04 — NO REVENUE VISIBILITY

No Revenue Visibility

You get monthly PDF reports showing impressions and clicks, but have no idea which ad keywords actually put money on your dispatch board.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work only with plumbing companies?

Yes, 100%. We work exclusively with plumbing contractors. We don't manage roofers, lawyers, or ecommerce stores. That focus lets us build and optimize campaigns based on real plumbing search behavior.

How much should a plumbing company spend on Google Ads?

Budgets typically depend on your service area population, van count, and competition. For a 1–5 van shop, $1,500 to $3,500/month is a common starting point to consistently generate qualified calls without spreading budget too thin.

Do I need a brand new website?

No. We typically build high-converting landing pages specifically for your Google Ads campaigns. Your existing website can stay exactly as it is, or we can improve it over time if you choose.

Do you require long-term contracts?

Never. We work strictly month-to-month. We keep clients because our campaigns produce booked jobs and fill dispatch boards, not because of legal contracts.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

No. We charge a flat monthly management fee. That means we have zero incentive to arbitrarily push you to spend more with Google. Your ad spend goes directly to Google.

How quickly can my campaigns launch?

We typically complete the initial audit, campaign build, negative keyword lists, tracking setup, and landing page within 14 to 21 business days.

What happens if I already have Google Ads running?

We'll audit your account first. We look for search term waste, negative keyword gaps, tracking errors, and landing page bottlenecks. We show you exactly where leads are leaking before making changes.

Can you manage Local Services Ads (LSA) too?

Yes. We manage Google Guaranteed LSA campaigns alongside Google Search Ads to ensure your company captures all high-intent local search demand.
