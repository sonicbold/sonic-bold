WHY PLUMBING ADS FAIL

You're paying Google. But is your phone actually ringing?

Most plumbing campaigns don't fail because homeowners aren't searching. They fail because budget leaks into bad searches, the landing page doesn't convert, calls get missed, or nobody tracks which leads actually become jobs.

01 — Wasted Clicks

Your budget goes toward DIY searches, job seekers and low-intent traffic instead of homeowners ready to hire.

02 — Weak Conversion

The homeowner clicks your ad, lands on a slow or confusing page, and goes back to Google.

03 — Missed Calls

A qualified lead calls while you're on a job or after hours. Nobody answers, so they call the next plumber.

04 — No Revenue Visibility

You get reports about impressions and clicks, but can't see which campaigns actually produced booked jobs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work only with plumbers?

Yes. We exclusively manage marketing for plumbing companies. We don't work with roofers, electricians, or dentists.

How much should a plumbing company spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your market, competition, and growth goals. During your audit, we'll review search volume in your specific service area and provide a realistic budget recommendation based on the cost per click.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. We work month-to-month. If we don't deliver results, you shouldn't be locked into paying us.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

Never. We charge a flat monthly management fee. We are not financially incentivized to blindly increase your Google Ads budget.

What happens if I already have Google Ads running?

We'll perform an audit of your existing account. If it's built correctly, we'll take over management and optimize it. If it's fundamentally broken, we'll rebuild the campaign structure properly.

How quickly can campaigns launch?

Generally, we can audit, build, and launch new campaigns and landing pages within 14 to 30 days depending on the scope.

Can you manage Local Services Ads too?

Yes. We can manage both traditional Google Search Ads (PPC) and Google Local Services Ads (Google Guaranteed).
