WHY PLUMBING ADS FAIL

You're paying Google. But is your phone actually ringing?

Most plumbing campaigns don't fail because homeowners aren't searching. They fail because budget leaks into DIY searches, Local Services Ads junk leads go undisputed, or the phone rings while every tech is under a sink.

01 — WASTED CLICKS

Wasted Clicks

Your budget goes toward DIY searches, job seekers, and low-intent traffic instead of homeowners ready to hire an emergency plumber.

02 — LSA JUNK

Undisputed LSA Leads

Google bills Local Services Ads leads that are spam, out of your area, or the wrong job. If nobody disputes them, the channel looks more expensive than it is.

03 — MISSED CALLS

Missed Calls

A qualified lead calls while your techs are under a sink or after 5 PM. Nobody answers, so they call the next plumber on Google within 30 seconds.

04 — VANITY REPORTS

Clicks Without Jobs

The monthly PDF is full of impressions and clicks. The dispatch board is not. You need someone managing the searches and LSA leads you can actually run.

Yes, 100%. We work exclusively with plumbing contractors. We don't manage roofers, lawyers, or ecommerce stores. That focus lets us build and optimize campaigns based on real plumbing search behavior.

Budgets typically depend on your service area population, van count, and competition. For a 1–5 van shop, $1,500 to $3,500/month is a common starting point to consistently generate qualified calls without spreading budget too thin.

Google Ads management and Local Services Ads management only, for US plumbing companies. You still pay Google for clicks and LSA leads. Websites, call tracking, Google Business Profile work, and landing pages are not included.

Never. We work strictly month-to-month. We keep clients because our campaigns produce booked jobs and fill dispatch boards, not because of legal contracts.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

No. After the free 30 days, we charge a flat monthly management fee. Your ad spend goes directly to Google.

Is the Google ad spend free too?

No. The free 30 days waives Sonic Bold's management fee for Google Ads and Local Services Ads. Clicks and LSA leads are still billed by Google.

How quickly can my campaigns launch?

Once we have account access, Google Ads and LSA management starts inside the free 30 days. There is no landing-page build or call-tracking setup in this offer.

Yes. LSA management is included with Google Ads management in the free 30 days. You still pay Google for the leads.
