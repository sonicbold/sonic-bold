Case study 05 Example scenario
WH

Water heater repair & replacement shop

Phoenix, AZ · 3 vans · LSA + Google Ads

+14 water heater installs in 90 days

Problem

Local Services Ads were live but full of spam, wrong-number, and out-of-area leads that nobody disputed, and the profile was missing water heater job types.

Fix

Cleaned up LSA categories and service area, added water heater job types, disputed invalid leads every week, and ran Search Ads for “water heater replacement” next to LSA.

31LSA leads disputed & credited
$74Cost per LSA lead
