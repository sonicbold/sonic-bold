Why most plumbing ads bleed money

If you've tried Google Ads before and got burned, you're not alone. The default settings on Google are designed to make Google money, not you.

  • Broad Match Keywords: Paying $40 a click for someone searching "how to fix a leaky faucet yourself."
  • No Negative Keywords: Wasting budget on searches for "plumbing supply store near me."
  • Leaky Funnels: Sending expensive clicks to a slow homepage instead of a dedicated, high-converting landing page.

Our Approach: Exclusive, High-Intent Calls

Local Services Ads (LSA)

We get you Google Guaranteed. You appear at the very top of the search results, and you only pay when a customer actually calls you, not just for a click. We handle the disputes for spam calls to save your budget.

Emergency Keyword Targeting

We focus your budget strictly on high-urgency keywords like "emergency plumber near me," "burst pipe repair," and "water heater leaking." High urgency means higher conversion rates and faster bookings.

Call Tracking & ROI

Every campaign is wired up with dynamic number insertion (DNI). We record every call and trace it back to the exact keyword that generated it, allowing us to ruthlessly cut ad spend that doesn't produce booked jobs.
