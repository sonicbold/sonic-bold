## What You Need to Do Next

### 1. Register for A2P 10DLC (CRITICAL)

Before sending ANY text messages:

1. **Go to The Campaign Registry**: https://www.thecampaignregistry.com
2. **Register your brand** (Sonic Bold LLC)
   - Use details in `A2P-10DLC-COMPLIANCE.md`
   - Submit EIN verification
   - Wait for approval (1-7 days)
3. **Register your campaign**
   - Use sample messages from compliance doc
   - Select "Mixed Marketing & Customer Care"
   - Wait for approval (1-5 days)
4. **Connect to your SMS provider** (Twilio, etc.)
   - Provide your TCR Campaign ID
   - Link your phone number to 10DLC

**DO NOT send SMS before 10DLC approval** - carriers will block you!

### 2. Update Your Terms & Privacy Policy

You'll need to update two pages with SMS disclosures:

**Privacy Policy** should include:
- How you collect phone numbers
- How SMS data is used
- Third-party SMS providers
- User rights (access, deletion, opt-out)
- Data retention (4 years minimum for TCPA)

**Terms of Service** should include:
- SMS terms of service section
- STOP/HELP keyword policies
- Message frequency expectations
- Cost disclosures
- Acceptable use

## Key Compliance Points to Remember

### ✅ DO:
- Get explicit written consent before ANY text message
- Honor STOP requests immediately (no delay)
- Send opt-out confirmation message
- Keep opt-in records for 4+ years
- Include business name in messages
- Provide STOP instructions
- Only send to people who checked the box

### ❌ DON'T:
- Pre-check the consent checkbox
- Send messages before 10DLC approval
- Send after user replies STOP
- Buy/rent phone number lists
- Use shared short codes for marketing
- Send from unregistered numbers
- Ignore high opt-out rates (indicates spam)
